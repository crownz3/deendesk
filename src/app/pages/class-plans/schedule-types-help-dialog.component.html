Schedule Types Guide

Detailed guide & examples for all Class Plan schedule types

Yearly Academic Plan

Yearly Plan (ஆண்டு கல்வித் திட்டம்)

Spans an entire academic year (e.g. June to April). Used for year-long course structures, annual syllabus milestones, and full-year class scheduling.

Real Example: "2026–2027 Full Academic Year Syllabus Plan for Aalim 1st Year."
Monthly Plan

Monthly Schedule (மாதாந்திரத் திட்டம்)

Structured for a specific calendar month. Ideal for tracking monthly syllabus targets, monthly unit tests, and monthly attendance summaries.

Real Example: "July 2026 Monthly Plan: Memorize Surah Al-Mulk & complete Fiqh Unit 2."
Weekly Schedule

Weekly Schedule (வாராந்திரத் திட்டம்)

Recurring weekly timetable on specified days (e.g. Mon, Wed, Fri). Best for standard regular classes and daily Madrasa sessions.

Real Example: "Maktab Section A — Mon/Wed/Fri 4:30 PM - 6:00 PM (Hifz & Tajweed)."
Term / Semester

Term / Semester Plan (பருவத் திட்டம்)

Structured for 3-month or 6-month academic terms (Quarterly / Half-Yearly curriculum goals).

Real Example: "1st Term Syllabus (June – October 2026) for Grade 5 Islamic Studies."
Date Range

Date Range Plan (தேதி வரம்புத் திட்டம்)

Runs continuously between a fixed Start Date and End Date. Ideal for short intensive courses or seasonal camps.

Real Example: "Ramadan Intensive Tajweed Camp (15 April to 15 May)."
One-Time Event

One-Time Event (ஒற்றை நிகழ்வு)

A single-day activity, exam, guest lecture, or special workshop.

Real Example: "Annual Quran Recitation Competition — 12 August 2026."
Custom Dates

Custom Selected Dates (தனிப்பயன் தேதிகள்)

Specific non-consecutive dates selected manually.

Real Example: "Revision Workshops on 5th, 15th, and 25th of every month."
